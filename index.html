The hidden risk of waiting: RM0 cost feels safe, but RM53K/month in lost Melaka revenue is compounding every month. After 6 months of waiting, we've "saved" RM17K–95K in pilot cost but lost RM318K+ in revenue we'll never recover — and the SURIA window closes permanently.
