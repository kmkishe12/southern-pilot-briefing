Decision Gates: Clear Exit Points

The pilot includes built-in checkpoints to continue, adjust, or stop — protecting downside risk.

Month 2 — First Performance Gate Contract SC: ≥1 NOC, ≥80% contact rate, CRM discipline. Interns: Activity targets met, coachability confirmed.
Continue Adjust Exit Individual / Pilot
Month 4 — Second Performance Gate Contract SC: Sustained 2–3 NOC/month, self-gen started. Interns: 10+ qualified leads/month, pitch-ready.
Continue Adjust Targets Exit Individual / Pilot
Month 6 — Final Decision Gate Full assessment: Convert Contract SC to full-time? Offer interns Junior SC role? Scale model to other markets?
Scale Repeat / Adjust Stop
Maximum downside exposure: If pilot fails at Month 2 gate, total cost = ~RM13,000 (2 months). This is less than one wrong full-time hire's first-month all-in cost.
